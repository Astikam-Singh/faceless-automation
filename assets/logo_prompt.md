# Logo Design Prompt for Ancient Mindset Lab

## Brand Identity
- **Brand Name:** Ancient Mindset Lab
- **Niche:** Stoic Philosophy & Mindset / Self-Improvement
- **Target Audience:** USA Adults 18-35 interested in personal development
- **Brand Personality:** Scientific, authoritative, philosophical yet modern, trustworthy

---

## Logo Prompt (Copy & Paste)

### For AI Image Generators (ChatGPT, Kling, Midjourney, DALL-E):

> **Create a professional minimalist logo for "Ancient Mindset Lab" - a stoic philosophy and mindset brand. The design should feature a stylized ancient Greek column or pillar integrated with a modern laboratory/science flask or DNA helix symbol. The style should be dark academia meets modern tech startup - elegant, mysterious, and authoritative.**
>
> **Color Palette:** Deep charcoal gray (#1A1A1A), Slate blue-gray (#4A5568), Antique gold accent (#C9A227), Pure white (#FFFFFF)
>
> **Style:** Geometric, clean lines, vector-style, minimalist. No gradients - flat design. The pillar should look carved from marble but with a subtle digital/tech undertone. The flask or helix should emerge from or intersect the pillar organically.
>
> **Composition:** Square or circular badge format. The text "Ancient Mindset Lab" should be in a modern serif font (like Playfair Display or Cormorant Garamond) below or around the icon. Include a small tagline: "Ancient Wisdom for Modern Minds" in smaller text below the main name.
>
> **Technical Requirements:** Transparent background, 1024x1024px minimum, vector-inspired look, suitable for use as watermark on videos, social media avatars, and YouTube channel art.

## Alternative Prompt Variations

### Option B - The Shield Design:
> **Create a sleek minimalist logo for "Ancient Mindset Lab" featuring a geometric shield shape. Inside the shield, show a stylized human brain profile silhouette in negative space, with a single ancient Greek column pillar rising from the brain. The shield should have sharp, angular edges - not curved. Color scheme: Navy blue (#1E3A5F), Gold (#D4AF37), White. Flat vector style, transparent background, 1024x1024px.**

### Option C - The Open Book/Flask:
> **Design a logo for "Ancient Mindset Lab" combining an open ancient scroll/book with a modern science flask. The flask sits atop the open book, with steam or energy rising from it in a subtle helix pattern. Color palette: Dark charcoal, muted gold, cream white. Elegant serif typography for the brand name. Minimalist flat design, suitable for YouTube watermark and social media. Transparent background.**

## Technical Specifications for Final Logo

Once you generate the logo, ensure it meets these requirements:

| Requirement | Specification |
|-------------|---------------|
| **File Format** | PNG with transparent background |
| **Resolution** | 1024x1024px minimum (for high-quality scaling) |
| **Usage** | Watermark (10-15% of video frame), YouTube profile, Instagram |
| **Colors** | Must work on both light AND dark backgrounds |
| **Style** | Flat, no gradients, clean edges |
