## Banner Design Prompt

### For AI Image Generators (ChatGPT, Kling, Midjourney):

> **Create a professional YouTube banner for "Ancient Mindset Lab" - a stoic philosophy and mindset brand. The banner should be 2560x1440 pixels with a dark academia aesthetic mixed with modern tech elements.**
>
> **Background:** Deep charcoal gradient (#0F0F11 to #1A1A1E) with subtle geometric patterns (Greek key motif or subtle mosaic pattern in very low opacity). Add a faint glowing light source from the top-left corner for depth.
>
> **Brand Elements:**
> - **Brand Name:** "Ancient Mindset Lab" in large, elegant serif font (Playfair Display or Cormorant Garamond) - white or antique gold color
> - **Logo:** Place the Ancient Mindset Lab logo (Greek pillar + flask concept) on the left side
> - **Tagline:** "Ancient Wisdom for Modern Minds" - smaller text in slate blue-gray
>
> **Visual Components:**
> - Right side: Abstract geometric shapes (hexagons, circles) in antique gold and slate blue
> - Center-left: Subtle visual of a marble pillar transitioning into digital circuit lines
> - Top-right corner: "Subscribe for Daily Wisdom" call-to-action (small, elegant)
>
> **Color Palette:**
> - Primary: Deep charcoal (#1A1A1A, #0F0F11)
> - Secondary: Slate blue-gray (#4A5568, #2D3748)
> - Accent: Antique gold (#C9A227, #B89624)
> - Text: Pure white (#FFFFFF)
>
> **Critical:**
> - Keep ALL important text and branding within the 650x115px center-safe area
> - The banner should look good when cropped on different devices
> - No critical elements near the top or bottom edges
> - Use flat design - no gradients on text
> - 300 DPI minimum for quality
>
> **Style:** Elegant, sophisticated, intellectual but modern. Avoid clutter. Negative space is your friend. The design should convey wisdom, authority, and trustworthiness.

## File Processing Checklist

Once you generate the banner:

1. **Verify dimensions:** 2560x1440 pixels
2. **Test safe area:** Make sure logo and text are within center 650x115 area
3. **Check file size:** Under 6 MB (compress if needed)
4. **Save as:** PNG (for quality) or JPEG (for smaller size)
5. **Filename:** `youtube_banner.jpg` or `youtube_banner.png`

## Upload to YouTube

1. Go to YouTube Studio
2. Click **Customization** → **Brand**
3. Click **Upload new image**
4. Select your banner file
5. Drag to adjust positioning (YouTube will show safe area preview)
6. Click **Save**
